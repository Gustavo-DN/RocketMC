# CLAUDE.md: Rocket4 (CC:Tweaked rocket for Minecraft Create Aeronautics / Sable)

Minecraft-only project: a 4-thruster cargo rocket flown by a CC:Tweaked computer.
It is a video-game delivery vehicle. It carries items (food that spoils) between bases in a Minecraft world and arrives on a rope net (upright in NET mode, nose-down in ARC mode) or in a water pool. Nothing here relates to real-world hardware.

Naming: the destination is where the cargo lands. Phases are IGNITION, ASCENT, EXPRESS (fast level cruise; its constants use the `AB_` prefix), TRANSFER, SINK, DIVE, RELEASE. Delivery modes are WATER, ARC and NET.
Read `ROCKET4_HANDOFF.md` first. It has all hardware facts, measured physics, API quirks, the code architecture, the flight history and the rejected designs.

## Files
- `Rocket4.lua`: flight program (the deliverable).
- `VectorMap.lua`: thruster mapping test (already run).
- `sim4.lua`: offline simulator; loads Rocket4.lua with stubbed CC/Sable APIs.
- `ROCKET4_HANDOFF.md`: full knowledge base.
- `Transport.lua`: server transport build of Rocket4 (Rocket4 itself is unchanged). NET only, no HTTP/Mission Control. Changes vs Rocket4:
  - Cruise: thrust-based altitude hold (`holdPitch`, pitch = asin((g + a + bias)/T)), cruising at start + climb (`AB_HOLD_CLIMBY`), faster pitch-over (AB_PITCH_RATE 30, AB_VDECEL 10).
  - Braking (`NET_FLIP`): at D(v)·1.08 + 30 blocks before the destination (D = 4.20·v + 0.00317·v²), FLIP (nose over the top at 90°/s, throttle 0.6), then RETRO (full throttle against the horizontal velocity, altitude held) until under 20 m/s, then TRANSFER at that height and SINK. FLIP is exempt from the attitude-error RECOVER check; RETRO gets an 8 s tilt grace. A recovery far from the destination returns to EXPRESS.
  - Security: launch and exit need the password (salted, 200-round SHA-256 in `SEC`; only the hash is stored), lockout after 3 wrong tries (30 s doubling, saved in `.transport.lock`), Ctrl+T ignored (os.pullEvent = os.pullEventRaw), REMOTE launches also need `mission.password`, `launch.log` audit, launch limits (Y, climb, max distance 60000), fuel check before launch and low-fuel brake-and-land when tank + engines < FUEL_RESERVE (both off: `FUEL_CHECK = false`, the server tank is infinite), double Backspace to cut engines. SELF_DELETE is on: the file deletes itself at start and the program stays in memory, returning to the locked launch screen after each landing (reinstall after a reboot or password exit). Optional owner check: list Minecraft names in `OWNERS` and attach an Advanced Peripherals Player Detector; launch/exit then also need an owner within OWNER_RANGE blocks (refused if no detector is attached).
  - `TransportStartup.lua` (rename to `startup.lua` on the computer): disables disk startup, reruns Transport after a crash, stops after a password exit (`.transport.exit`) or when Transport.lua is missing (self-deleted).
  - Simulator (real-matched VDRAG physics): 1,500 / 7,000 / 12,000 / 20,000 blocks in 60 / 93 / 108 / 137 s vs Rocket4 NET 73 / 110 / 137 / 165 s, all within 0.3 blocks. `sim4.lua` takes `PASS=` for the mission password.
- `FlightTest.lua`: standalone automatic test flight (own copy of the steering, no dependency on Rocket4): climb 200, level off, full-throttle cruise with thrust-based altitude hold (pitch = asin((g + a)/T)) until speed plateaus, then FLIP (nose swings over the top from 20° up forward to 20° up backward at up to 90°/s, throttle 0.6), RETRO burn (full throttle against the horizontal velocity, altitude held by thrust) until below 20 m/s, upright descent and landing. Writes `flighttest.log` (0.25 s rows incl. nose yaw vs start heading and horizontal speed) and `flighttest.txt` (per-stage summary).
- `ManeuverTest.lua`: standalone brake-distance test (same steering as FlightTest). Climbs 200, then brake trials from 120, 180, 240 m/s and top speed: accelerate, flip + retro burn until under 20 m/s, record distance and time, then accelerate the other way for the next trial. Writes `maneuvertest.txt` (BRAKE TABLE: start speed, total distance/time, flip and burn parts, altitude change, max spin) and `maneuvertest.log`. Simulator prediction (VDRAG real-matched physics): 180 m/s ≈ 810 blocks / 6.6 s, 240 ≈ 1,150 / 7.6 s, 317 ≈ 1,630 / 8.9 s.
- `ARCManeuverTest.lua`: standalone dive test with Rocket4's ARC steering (`approachDir`, throttle 0.5, NOSE_OFFSET 12.7, DRAG 0.13). Climbs to +1300, then per trial: hold the trial speed (0 = top speed), put an imaginary destination 450 blocks lower at start distance drop/tan(angle) + v·lead (Rocket4's ARC rule, lead 4 s), dive until the nose tip reaches its height, record miss, arrival speed, path angle, nose tilt, then pull out (nose to vertical at 90°/s, full throttle) and record the height lost. Trials: top speed at 30°, 200 m/s at 45°, 200 m/s at 60°. Skips remaining trials below 12,000 mB fuel. Writes `arcmaneuvertest.txt` (DIVE TABLE) and `arcmaneuvertest.log`. `sim4.lua` takes `FUEL=` for the reported tank amount.

The real-matched simulator predicted 450 for that pull-out. Rocket4's ARC start rule is accurate at top speed.

- `SmallTest.lua`: test for the small single-thruster rocket (works with any number of thrusters, all driven together). Lists peripherals and methods, mass and inertia, steps the gimbal on the ground (lag), ramps throttle to liftoff, then measures how the gimbal turns the rocket (0.3 s doublets on each axis, least squares: angular acceleration = M·gimbal + bias) and flies with that map: climbs to +8, hovers 4 s (hover throttle), 5° tilt steps on each axis, lands at 1.5 m/s. Cuts if tilt > 40° or spin > 4 rad/s. Writes `smalltest.txt` (summary) and `smalltest.log` (rows). Simulator check: lands upright at 2 m/s, also with the thruster mounted rotated 90°.
- Real SmallTest (2026-10-10, `logs/`): small rocket, 1 thruster on `bottom`, tank on `top`. Mass 5.5, inertia pitch/yaw 15.6, roll 0.92. Liftoff at throttle 0.229 (ramp lag), hover 0.135 → 77.6 m/s² per unit throttle (very overpowered: one throttle step of 1/15 is ±5 m/s²). Gimbal targets are also quantized to 1/15 (0.5 → 0.533). Thruster getters (getVectorX etc.) return instantly, unlike setters.
- `Rocket.lua`: program for the small single-thruster rocket (thruster on `bottom`, tank on `top`; mass 5.5, see the SmallTest results). Uploaded by the user, then reduced to two modes (old ARC/NET code removed):
  - WATER (was SPLASH): IGNITION → ASCENT (climb, gradual turn toward the target) → CRUISE (`diveDir` proportional navigation at full throttle, 70% inside 300 blocks) → TOUCHDOWN when the nose tip reaches the target or stops (speed < 2 for 1 s).
  - SHUTTLE: released from a carrier in flight. LAUNCH arms it (no countdown) and it waits for release: mass drops below 70% of the armed mass (it left the carrier's sub-level), free fall (vertical acceleration < -6 m/s² for 0.4 s, after 1 s armed; `REL_FREEFALL`), a redstone signal on any side, or ENTER. Then SEPARATE (engine off 0.5 s), then the same CRUISE and TOUCHDOWN as WATER, throttle ramping from 0.15 at 1.0/s. Plan is computed at release.
  - Changes vs the upload: all sublevel reads and thruster writes batched per tick (`tick()`; the upload made 5 sequential calls, ~250 ms per loop), THRUST_ACC 77.6 (measured), KD 0.6, rate feed-forward of the commanded direction in the D term (FF_TAU 0.2, FF_MAX 1.5), error capped at ERR_MAX 1.2 rad, straight-behind case handled (the upload never turned when the target was exactly behind the nose), integral ATT_KI 1.0 within 20°, direction held for the last 0.4 s (HOLD_TGO).
  - Simulator `simrocket.lua` (`lua5.1 simrocket.lua Rocket.lua`; env MODE TX TY TZ CLIMB GROUND, SHUTTLE: SY SV SACC REL COAST NOMASS; physics: TACC AX AY CPL DRAG KAERO TQMAX HEAD). Uses the measured gimbal map, 1/15 quantization of throttle and gimbal, 0.23 s gimbal lag. Results: WATER 0.3–7.6 blocks over 11 cases (150–5,000 blocks), SHUTTLE 0.6–7.5 blocks over 10 cases including a target straight below. Fails only with unlimited sail torque (KAERO 0.03, no cap) or TQMAX 5 with KAERO 0.03: the flip right after release while still climbing fast. The small rocket's real aero is unknown.

## Hard rules for Rocket4.lua
- Lua 5.1 (CC:Tweaked CraftOS 1.9). No comments, no `print()`. Clean code.
- All tunables go in the `local C = { ... }` table. Every `C.X` used must be defined there.
- Keep upvalues per function under 60 and locals under 200 (current max is 28 upvalues).
- All peripheral and sublevel calls in the flight loop go through the batched `E.io()` (one `parallel.waitForAll`). Never add sequential peripheral calls to the loop: each costs 50 ms.
- Never cut engines at altitude. Only the ground rule cuts (tilt > 60° within CUT_LOW of the ground).
- Recovery must never add climbing thrust.
- NET route: EXPRESS → BRAKE → TRANSFER → SINK (upright soft landing).
- ARC route (ARC_FAST, default): EXPRESS → DIVE with no braking. The descent starts when the line of sight to the destination is ARC_DIVE_ANG below horizontal (plus ARC_DIVE_T lead) and flies nose-first at ARC_DIVE_THR with `approachDir`. A recovery returns to DIVE. The net is very large, so speed matters more than precision. With ARC_FAST = false, ARC brakes (BRAKE_STYLE) and uses the slow `arcDir` DIVE.
- Deliver the finished file; the user copies it into the game.

Baseline to keep or beat:
- ARC (ARC_FAST): nose tip within about 1–10 blocks in most runs, arriving nose-down at 100–175 m/s. 400 blocks ≈ 25 s, 7,000 ≈ 62 s, 12,000 ≈ 83 s. The simulator prints `tip_xz`, `impV` and `landTilt`.
- Previous upright ARC (commit 4d8760b), for reference: 15 of 16 within 1.4 blocks, worst 2.8.
- NET: 0.2–0.4 blocks, no CUT.

Any `TERMERR` line is a runtime error (the program's top-level pcall hides it otherwise).
